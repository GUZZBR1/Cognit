Before you begin

This pilot records your challenge, answer, whether it was correct, display duration, and response time. It does not collect your name, contact details, mouse movement, or other raw interaction data. Records stay in this browser until exported or deleted. The research team deletes retained exports within 30 days.

You may stop at any time. Use “Withdraw and delete” to erase this participant’s records held by this page. If you have already downloaded a file, contact the researcher using the contact details on your study invitation to request deletion of those copies.

Raw data capture is off and unavailable in this pilot. No data is sent to a server.
